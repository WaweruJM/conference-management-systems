ABSTRACT SUBMISSION GUIDELINES

===================================================
ABSTRACT TITLE
===================================================

1. The title, aligned with the conference theme and sub-themes should:
   a. Be a concise statement of the main topic.
   b. Contain a maximum of 20 words (Capitalize each word).
   c. Be in Times New Roman, font size 12.

===================================================
CONFERENCE SUB-THEMES
===================================================

2. Indicate the sub-theme that aligns with your paper.

===================================================
AUTHOR(S) INFORMATION
===================================================

3. Provide the following details:
   a. Names of author(s) with one of the authors clearly designated as the
      corresponding author by use of an Asterisk *.
      The principal author should appear first.
   b. Contact details of the corresponding author (Email addresses and phone
      numbers).
   c. Authors' affiliated institutions (Affiliations should include department,
      hospital/university/institution).

===================================================
ABSTRACT BODY
===================================================

4. Submit your abstract through the SCMS platform.

   a. Be aligned with the conference theme and sub-themes.
   b. Be a summary of key aspects of the manuscript.
   c. Be in Times New Roman, font size 12, single spaced and NOT EXCEEDING
      300 WORDS.
   d. NOT include any citation.

   e. For ORIGINAL PAPERS, the abstract should include a few sentences
      from each of the following sections:

      (1) Background: Crucial background, be as precise as possible to enable
          the readers to understand your research from the onset.
      (2) Objective: Aligned with the research problem statement/gap your
          research is filling, must be SMART.
      (3) Methods: Study design, study population, sampling, data collection
          methods, data analysis etc.
      (4) Results: Summary of major findings, with p values as appropriate;
          aligned with your objectives.
      (5) Conclusion: Brief overall interpretation from the results, key take
          home or the answer to the gap the research set out to fill.
      (6) Recommendation: Broader implications, future research.

   f. For CASE REPORTS or CASE SERIES, the sub-headings are:

      (1) Background: Provide a concise rationale for why the case
          (or series) is worth reporting. Explain what is already known
          (or not known) about the condition and what makes your case notable
          (rare presentation, new complication, novel management, etc.)
      (2) Objective: Clearly state the aim of your case report or series;
          what you intend to show or highlight through this report.
      (3) Case Presentation: Describe the case(s) in a logical/chronological
          order, but be concise. If a case series, summarise each case
          individually (for <4) and emphasise similarities/differences.
          If >4 cases, summarize their similarities, then bring out their
          uniqueness, compare and contrast to clearly communicate the
          author's overarching theme/rationale of presenting the cases.
      (4) Case Discussion: Interpret the case(s), compare with existing
          literature, and highlight what is new, unexpected, or noteworthy.
      (5) Conclusion: Summarise the main clinical message or takeaway from
          your case(s).
      (6) Recommendation: Provide practical suggestions based on your
          findings. These might relate to future research, clinical practice,
          or policy.

   g. Disclosure Statement: Funding sources of the research or any parties
      that may portend conflict of interest with the authors conducting/
      writing the study, should be declared with their roles indicated.
      If none exists, this still needs to be indicated as "no disclosures
      to make" or "no conflict of interest to declare" at the end of the
      abstract.

   h. Key Words: 5 words that stand out throughout the paper to be listed
      in a sentence separated by comma.

   NOTE: The word limit of 300 is only for the BODY of the abstract.
   It does not include the topic, authors, qualifications, affiliations,
   institutions, key words (if any) and disclosures.

===================================================
FILE FORMAT REQUIREMENTS (for uploaded documents)
===================================================

- Format: Microsoft Word (.doc, .docx) only.
- Maximum file size: 2 MB.
- Document must NOT contain author names (double-blind review).
- Font: Times New Roman, size 12, single spaced.
